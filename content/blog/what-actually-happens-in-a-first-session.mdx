Most people who book a PlusOne have never done anything like it. Their only real question isn't about price or safety — it's *"but what do we actually talk about for three hours?"*

Fair question. So here's the unglamorous truth of how a first session runs.

## Before you meet

You and your companion get the same brief: the occasion, the time, the public place, and anything you've asked us to avoid. Companions read it before they reply to you, so they already know it's your cousin's sangeet and that you left the gift at home.

You'll get a message when they leave. Not a live location tracker, just a heads-up so you're not standing around wondering.

## The first ten minutes

They arrive a few minutes early. Every companion is briefed to open with something normal — a comment about the venue, a joke about the parking, a question about the function. Not a scripted "hello client, I am your companion." That would be weird for both of you.

You'll also hear the ground rules restated out loud, once, casually:

- Public place, always.
- Platonic, always.
- If either of you feels uncomfortable, the session can end early, no argument, no refund drama.

That last one applies to you too. You're allowed to leave.

## The middle bit

Here's what nobody tells you: it's just a conversation. Your companion has done this enough to know when to talk and — more importantly — when not to.

Typical topics, in no particular order: work, the food, films, whatever's on your mind, the relatives, why your friend is marrying someone called Bunty. If you don't want to talk, they'll carry it. If you want to talk about something heavy, they'll listen without giving you unsolicited advice.

They will not:

- Ask for your Instagram.
- Ask for your number.
- Follow you around after the session.
- Oversell themselves for a rebooking mid-conversation.

If they're good, you'll forget they're being paid for about twenty minutes. That's the goal.

## The ending

Companions wind down five minutes early and confirm what you actually came for — that you had a decent time and got through the thing you were dreading. Payment's already settled, so there's no awkward money moment at the door.

Then they leave. That's it. No lingering, no "text me sometime", no follow-up unless *you* book them again.

## What happens afterwards

You'll get one message from us asking how it went, and that's the only message we'll send unless you rebook. Your companion doesn't keep your number, and you don't keep theirs.

If it went well, book the same person again — the second session is always easier, because the small-talk tax is already paid.
